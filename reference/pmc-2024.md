# PMC SRTR studies: 2024 publication search

A growing source catalog for reviewers and coding agents. **89 direct or linked
SRTR studies** retained in a first-pass relevance screen; five have
annotations following main-article reads. Inclusion is not endorsement of a
method or validation of an sRtr function. No article text is stored here.

## Search and reading status

Searched and screened **2026-11-14** using NCBI PMC ESearch/EFetch:
`(SRTR OR "Scientific Registry of Transplant Recipients") AND 2024[Publication Date]`.
[Repeat search](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=%28SRTR+OR+%22Scientific+Registry+of+Transplant+Recipients%22%29+AND+2024%5BPublication+Date%5D).
The search returned 755 unique PMC records. This file contains the 89 classified
as direct or linked SRTR studies, excluding aggregate-only studies, related
registries without confirmed SRTR use, background, and unresolved records.
This is a search-defined starting set, not an exhaustive bibliography.

Publication-year matching can include online-first and print years that differ;
it does not mean the study analyzed transplants performed in 2024. The index
preserves the retrieved publication years. Organ/topic labels are preliminary
navigation aids, chiefly inferred from titles, not verified eligibility criteria.
“Screened only” means title plus available abstract/targeted source passages,
not an exhaustive full-text appraisal. Linked studies may need external data
unavailable in standard SAFs.

## Annotation fields

Use the following labels after reading a paper:

- **Organ / population:** organs, cohort, and unit of analysis.
- **Files needed:** distinguish paper-reported files from inferred record families;
  identify external data and unverified release-specific mappings.
- **Methods — [label]:** cohort/linkage, exposure, outcome/time origin/censoring,
  missingness, estimation, or validation, as relevant.
- **Function relevance — review / modify / propose:** what a reviewer or coding
  agent can learn for existing or potential function families. These are reading
  leads, not findings about current code or approved implementation decisions.
- **Cautions / next verification:** limitations, conflicting definitions, and
  source details that must be resolved before implementation.
- **Read / locators:** date, reading scope, and source sections/tables.

Exact field codes and file mappings require the applicable dictionary/release;
none of the trial annotations validates them. Keep summaries compact and link
the source. Record any resulting package proposals separately from this catalog.

## Trial annotations

### Multiorgan linkage

[Long-term outcomes of patients with kidney and intestine-containing graft co-transplantation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12851315/)

- **Organ / population:** intestine–kidney; first intestinal transplant, preoperative dialysis, and prior kidney waitlisting; 54 patients.
- **Files needed — reported:** `tx_in`, `tx_ki`, `txf_in`; cross-organ linkage by `PERS_ID`. Release-specific keys and field meanings remain unverified.
- **Methods — linkage / exposure:** earliest intestine transplant retained; kidney transplant within seven days classified as simultaneous, later as delayed.
- **Methods — outcomes / missingness:** death-inclusive intestinal graft failure and mortality; 2,000-day horizon. Analyses also restart follow-up at kidney transplant or censor at kidney transplant. EPTS approximated with missing diabetes treated as absent and dialysis duration set to zero.
- **Function relevance — review / modify:** inspect person-versus-transplant linkage, time origins, composite endpoints, and missing-input handling. **Propose:** reusable cross-organ episode classification is a candidate function family to investigate, with explicit timing rules.
- **Cautions / next verification:** abstract starts in 1994, Methods in 1995. Seven days and EPTS substitutions are study choices, not validated defaults. Reviewer inference: delayed-transplant groups raise survival-selection/immortal-time concerns; restarting the clock does not establish causal benefit.
- **Read / locators:** 2026-11-14; abstract, complete main text, Table 1 and figure captions; Methods, Results, Discussion. Figure images and supplementary material not appraised.

### Paired kidneys

[Cold Ischemia Time and Delayed Graft Function in Kidney Transplantation: A Paired Kidney Analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11338744/)

- **Organ / population:** kidney; 2015–2019 deceased donors with both kidneys transplanted separately; donor pair is the comparison unit.
- **Files needed — inferred:** donor-linked kidney transplant records with ischemia time, pumping, recipient covariates and DGF; outcome/follow-up information for 90-day graft failure. Main text does not specify exact SAF filenames.
- **Methods — cohort / exposure:** exclude pairs with missing ischemia time/DGF, identical ischemia times, or differences exceeding 20 hours; assign shorter/longer ischemia within donor.
- **Methods — outcome / estimation:** DGF is dialysis during the first posttransplant week. Describe four paired outcome patterns; robust Poisson models restrict to DGF-discordant pairs. Sensitivity analyses address pumping and same-center transplantation.
- **Function relevance — review / modify:** inspect donor-link integrity, pair exclusions, DGF definitions, and analytic denominators. **Propose:** paired-organ assembly and concordance summaries are candidate function families to investigate.
- **Cautions / next verification:** discordant-pair estimates are not whole-cohort risks. Methods and table labels differ at ischemia-bin boundaries. Imputation details require the supplement; pumping selection can confound associations. Do not infer donor-clustered variance from “robust” alone.
- **Read / locators:** 2026-11-14; abstract, complete main text, Tables 1–4 and figure captions; Methods and Discussion. Figure images and supplementary material not appraised.

### Liver waitlist

[Association of nonstandardized model for end-stage liver disease score exceptions with waitlist mortality in adult liver transplant candidates](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11772122/)

- **Organ / population:** adult liver-only candidates listed June 2016–April 2022; follow-up through March 2024.
- **Files needed — inferred:** candidate registrations, longitudinal status/MELD/exception records, transplant and death information. Exact filenames unreported in main text. Exception narratives are unavailable in SAFs.
- **Methods — cohort / history:** earliest simultaneous registration; transferred registrations combined. Laboratory MELD and exception-related score increases vary over time; status carried through temporary inactivity.
- **Methods — outcomes / estimation:** mortality includes recorded deaths within six months after delisting, not automatic death assignment for deterioration removals. Cause-specific mixed-effects Cox models treat deceased-donor transplant and death as competing events; additional censoring includes status 1, living-donor transplant, multiorgan listing and standardized exceptions. Candidate-level bootstrap for concordance.
- **Function relevance — review / modify:** inspect registration consolidation, interval construction, death ascertainment and outcome-specific censoring. **Propose:** longitudinal waitlist/status preparation is a candidate function family to investigate.
- **Cautions / next verification:** reconcile six-month post-delisting mortality capture with “last observed” censoring before translating endpoints. Supplemental construction details remain unread. Proportional-hazards violations and possible informative censoring limit interpretation; a cause-specific model alone does not establish causality.
- **Read / locators:** 2026-11-14; abstract, complete main text, Table 1 and figure captions; Methods §§2.1–2.4 and Limitations. Figure images and supplementary material not appraised.
