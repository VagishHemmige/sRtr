# PMC SRTR studies: 2024 publication search

A growing catalog of papers that use SRTR data, for reviewers and coding
agents. Entries follow the "Literature annotations" section of
[`README.md`](README.md#literature-annotations). Inclusion is not endorsement
of a paper's methods. No article text is stored here.

## Search and screening

- Search string: `(SRTR OR "Scientific Registry of Transplant Recipients") AND 2024[Publication Date]`
  in PMC ([repeat search](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=%28SRTR+OR+%22Scientific+Registry+of+Transplant+Recipients%22%29+AND+2024%5BPublication+Date%5D)).
- Run date: 2026-11-14 (NCBI PMC ESearch/EFetch).
- Records returned: 755 (*reported*). Records kept: 89 (*reported*). The
  screening log is not in the repository.
- Kept: studies that analyze SRTR data directly or through a linkage.
  Excluded: aggregate-only studies, related registries without confirmed SRTR
  use, background citations, and unresolved records (*reported*; counts by
  reason not recorded).
- This is a search-defined starting set, not a complete bibliography.

`[Publication Date]` can match an online-first year and a print year that
differ, so the index keeps both years where they differ; it does not mean the
study analyzed 2024 transplants. Organ labels are navigation aids, mostly
taken from titles. SRTR role is `Not recorded` where the screen did not record
it.

### PMC12851315

Intestine–kidney timing ·
[Long-term outcomes of patients with kidney and intestine-containing graft co-transplantation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12851315/) ·
doi:10.1016/j.intf.2024.100005 · SAF analysis · 1995–2023 (abstract says
1994) · MT, 2026-11-14

- **Population / unit:** first intestine transplant, on dialysis and listed
  for kidney before it; 54 people. Unit: person. [reported §Patients;
  Results]
- **Files and fields:** `tx_in`, `tx_ki`, `txf_in`, `PERS_ID` [reported
  §Study design]; `CAN_DIAL` [reported §Patients] is in CAND_KIPA, CAND_LIIN
  and CAND_THOR, not TX_IN [dictionary: `sRtr::dictionary` at 8bc630d].
  Kidney waitlisting needs candidate registration records; the paper names no
  source [inference].
- **Data needs:** link organ files by person; keep the earliest intestine
  transplant; group by days from intestine to kidney transplant (seven-day
  window); graft failure includes death; 2,000-day horizon; second time
  origin at kidney transplant; delayed group also censored at kidney
  transplant and compared with the no-kidney group. [reported §Study design;
  Patients; Statistical analysis; Results] Tags: `cross-organ-link`,
  `repeat-transplant`, `candidate-registration`, `follow-up-horizon`,
  `derived-score`.
- **Data pitfalls:** the paper does not say which group day 7 falls in, or how
  a kidney transplant before the intestine transplant was handled; it uses
  "within" and "following" seven days [unclear §Patients]. The delayed group
  waited a median of 3,087 days for the kidney [reported §Results], so time
  before that date cannot count as delayed-group time at risk [inference].
  `CAN_DIAL` was recorded for 68% of intestine recipients; diabetes and
  dialysis start were too sparse for EPTS, which was computed with the OPTN
  online calculator using substituted values (no diabetes when missing;
  dialysis start set to 0 years for all patients, as written) [reported
  §Patients; Statistical analysis].
- **Locators:** Methods (Study design; Patients and study groups;
  Statistical analysis); Results.

### PMC11338744

Paired kidneys, cold ischemia and delayed graft function ·
[Cold Ischemia Time and Delayed Graft Function in Kidney Transplantation: A Paired Kidney Analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11338744/) ·
doi:10.1097/TP.0000000000005006 · SAF analysis · donors 2015–2019 · MT+T,
2026-11-14

- **Population / unit:** deceased donors with both kidneys transplanted, each
  to a recipient of that kidney alone; 25,831 pairs after exclusions. Unit:
  donor pair. [reported §Methods; Results]
- **Files and fields:** file names not reported. Kidney transplant records
  linked by donor, with cold ischemia time, pumping, delayed graft function
  (DGF), recipient covariates and 90-day graft failure [reported §Methods].
  Matching fields: `DONOR_ID`, `REC_COLD_ISCH_TM`, `REC_FIRST_WEEK_DIAL`,
  `REC_RECEIVED_ON_PUMP`, `DON_LF_KI_PUMP`, `DON_RT_KI_PUMP` and
  `REC_TX_PROCEDURE_TY` (format TXPROC: left, right, en bloc) are in TX_KI
  [dictionary: `sRtr::dictionary` at 8bc630d].
- **Data needs:** pair two recipients by donor; restrict to single-organ
  kidney recipients; exclude pairs missing cold ischemia time or DGF, with
  equal times, or differing by more than 20 hours; label the longer and
  shorter kidney within each pair; DGF is dialysis in the first week after
  transplant; KDPI computed from KDRI with the 2015 scaling factor; center of
  each transplant, for a same-center subset. [reported §Methods; Results]
  Tags: `donor-pair`, `derived-score`.
- **Data pitfalls:** the paper does not say which pumping field it used
  [unclear §Methods]. Donor-side pump fields are recorded per left and right
  kidney, so using them needs the kidney each recipient received, and en
  bloc transplants do not split into a pair [inference]. DGF is binary in the
  data; the number of dialysis sessions is not recorded, nor is pump
  duration or settings [reported §Discussion].
  Pre-transplant dialysis start was missing for 51 recipients on dialysis
  [reported Table 2 footnote]. Handling of other missing values is in
  Supplementary Methods (not read).
- **Locators:** Methods; Statistical Analysis; Results; Discussion; Tables
  1–4 captions and footnotes.

### PMC11772122

Liver waitlist, non-standardized MELD exceptions ·
[Association of nonstandardized model for end-stage liver disease score exceptions with waitlist mortality in adult liver transplant candidates](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11772122/) ·
doi:10.1016/j.ajt.2024.09.028 · SAF analysis · listed June 2016–April 2022,
follow-up to March 2024 · MT, 2026-11-14

- **Population / unit:** adult (over 17 at listing) liver-only candidates;
  excludes inactive at listing, Status 1, standardized exception at listing,
  and candidates already on another organ's waiting list; 60,322 candidates
  at 135 centers. Unit: candidate. [reported §2.1; §3.1]
- **Files and fields:** SRTR SAFs; file names not reported [reported §2.1].
  Needs candidate registrations, status history with laboratory and
  allocation MELD over time, and exception records that separate
  standardized from non-standardized exceptions [inference]. Files with
  these roles exist: CAND_LIIN, STATHIST_LIIN, MPEXCEPT [dictionary:
  `sRtr::dictionary` at 8bc630d]. Written exception narratives are not in
  the SAFs [reported §4].
- **Data needs:** one record per candidate (earliest of simultaneous
  registrations; transfers combined, with waitlist time counted from the
  first registration); time-varying laboratory and allocation MELD across
  all status updates, with status carried through inactive periods; deaths
  on the list and up to 6 months after removal; deceased-donor transplant as
  a competing event; censoring at Status 1, living-donor transplant, being
  added to another organ's waiting list, first standardized exception, or
  last observed on the list; policy era split at the National Liver Review
  Board start (administrative censoring on 14 May 2019); UNOS region and
  listing center. [reported §2.1–2.4; Limitations] Tags:
  `candidate-registration`, `status-history`, `post-removal-death`,
  `death-source`, `policy-era`, `geography`.
- **Data pitfalls:** counting deaths up to 6 months after removal conflicts
  with censoring at "last observed on the list"; the paper does not say how
  they fit [unclear §2.3]. Methods keep the earliest of simultaneous
  registrations, while the flow text counts 1,841 as excluded for listing at
  several centers; the unit of that count is not stated [unclear §2.1;
  §3.1]. How standardized and non-standardized exceptions were told apart is
